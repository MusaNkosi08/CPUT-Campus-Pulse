ABOUT THE SIGNAL

Campus problems,
made visible.

Campus Pulse is a real-time issue layer for CPUT District Six — made to be checked, not just submitted.

◉

THE WAZE PARALLEL

Reports decay
without proof.

When an issue is reported, its pin pulses. Students can say “Still there” or “It's fixed”. No confirmation for 48 hours? It turns stale and dims. The map tells the truth as it changes.

✦

THE HUMAN LOOP

One tap keeps
a signal alive.

Confirmation counts are visible on every issue. Transparency is not a feature bolted on later — it is the product language.
